# Consulti — B2B + Local Database Campaign

**Target:** Marketing agencies and PR companies
**Structure:** 4 emails · 4 variants on E1 (2 local, 2 B2B) · 1 variant per database on E2, E3, E4
**Cadence:** Day 1 → wait 2 → wait 3 → wait 4 · plain text ON · open tracking OFF

---

## What we're offering

Consulti is a **self-serve lead data platform** — you filter and pull the lists yourself, no done-for-you service.

This campaign runs **two parallel tracks**:

- **LOCAL** — 5M+ local businesses with the owner's direct email address, searchable by city and type of business. The wedge: most business listings only carry a general inbox, so outreach never reaches the person who decides.
- **B2B** — 10M+ business contacts, decision makers at companies rather than general inboxes, kept up to date. Narrows to both the kind of company and the person inside it.

## Lead magnets

1. **Custom list** built for their city and business type (LOCAL) or their target companies (B2B) — the primary ask
2. **No cost month of pro access** so they can pull their own
3. **~20 prebuilt B2B lists** at `consulti.ai/free-leads`, no signup (E3 B2B only). Note: this page is **B2B only** — never describe it as local or creator data.

## Copy rules

- Say **"no cost"**, never "free" (spam-filter trigger). Only exception is the `/free-leads` URL.
- The platform is **paid** — only the gifted month is free. Frame as "I can share a no cost month", never "it's free".
- **No em dashes.**
- **Do not invent pain points.** Earlier drafts claimed agencies scrape Google Maps by hand and rely on referrals. Neither holds, and both read as condescending to people who market for a living. Pains must be real or hedged as a question.
- Not all recipients are agencies — avoid assuming agency workflows.
- Every email ends on a **yes / interested question**. E3 LOCAL deliberately withholds the gift link so interest becomes a tracked reply instead of a silent click.

## Notes

**V1 splits its magnets differently to V2–V4.** The no cost month sits in the body and the sample list moved to the PS, because V1's question-first structure needed the larger offer inside the flow. V2–V4 keep the month in the PS. Deliberate, not drift.

**Track separation.** Run LOCAL variants to segments that sell to local businesses, B2B variants to the rest, or split the whole list and treat the two tracks as the primary A/B.

## Pre-launch checklist

1. Flip **plain text ON** in the Bison UI (the API cannot set it)
2. Attach the lead list, then unpause
3. Mailboxes: vetted senders only — Connected, warmup on, warmup score 75+, bounce under 3%
4. Confirm fulfilment capacity for custom list requests before scaling volume
