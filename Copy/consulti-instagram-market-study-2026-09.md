# Consulti Instagram Database — Market Study
**Date:** 2026-09-16
**Question:** who would pay most for a database of Instagram creators with emails, and how should we pitch them?
**Inputs:** desk research (web, cited below), competitor pricing pages, Consulti DB counts measured 2026-08-19 (not re-run: no API key in this session), the Heinz call (2026-09-10).

---

## TL;DR

1. **Sell to businesses that make money FROM creators, not brands that pay creators.** Our contactable Instagram slice skews big (median ~650k followers) and has broken geo. That is a bad fit for brands wanting US micro-influencers, and a great fit for service businesses whose ideal client is a 300k–5M creator.
2. **Top three segments to test:** clipping agencies, creator monetization / "backend" operators, creator talent managers.
3. **Nobody owns this buyer.** Every incumbent (Modash, HypeAuditor, Upfluence, GRIN, Aspire…) is built for brands running influencer campaigns. No one markets creator data to clippers, editors, growth agencies, or talent managers.
4. **Pitch = money per closed creator, not list size.** "One creator = a $5k/mo retainer" (clipping), "one signed creator ≈ $24k/yr commission" (talent), "one launch = $100k+" (backend).
5. **Price anchor:** Modash is $199/mo for **150 email unlocks (~$1.33/email)** and wants you sending from its inbox. Consulti = bulk export straight into Bison/Instantly.

## 3. Competitive landscape

Three tiers:
1. **Brand suites** — $199 to $2,000+/mo, rationed email unlocks, want you sending from their app.
2. **Contact-data vendors** — cheaper per contact, pay-per-unlock, variable quality.
3. **Scrapers / freelancers** — near-free per email, but DIY, messy, risk to your own IG account.

Consulti sits between tiers 2 and 3.

| Tool | Main buyer | Entry price | ~Cost per email | Notes / complaints |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Modash** | Brands (Shopify) | $199/mo = 150 unlocks; $499 = 400 | **~$1.33** | Public emails only; hits credit caps; thin on small creators *(official pricing)* |
| **Creator.co** | Brands | $199–$299/mo = 100 contact credits, 3-mo min | ~$1.99 | Very few credits *(official pricing)* |
| **HypeAuditor** | Brands, enterprise agencies | ~$299/mo billed annually (~$3k/yr) | n/a | Missing/outdated emails, expensive *(3rd-party pricing)* |
| **Upfluence** | Mid-market brands/agencies | ~$478–$1,276/mo, 12-mo contract | n/a | Trustpilot 3.4: lock-in, hard to cancel *(3rd-party)* |
| **GRIN** | DTC brands | $399/mo self-serve; enterprise $30k+/yr | n/a | Relationship mgmt, not discovery *(3rd-party)* |
| **Aspire** | Larger brands | ~$2k/mo, annual only | n/a | No monthly, no trial *(3rd-party)* |
| **Influencer Hero** | Brands; agencies pay per brand | $649/mo, 3-mo min | n/a | Price *(3rd-party)* |
| **Influencers.club** | Agencies, SaaS, API | $140/mo starter | ~$0.28–$0.42 | Credits don't roll over; <10k-follower emails stale |

| **IGLeads** | Cold emailers | $59/mo = 10k emails | ~$0.005 | Slow scrapes (3–4 hrs / 300 emails), bad "unlimited" data |
| **Apify / PhantomBuster** | Technical users | Pay per result / $69+/mo | $1.20–$6 per 1k | DIY, actors break, account risk |
| **Fiverr gigs** | Tiny buyers | $5–$25/gig | varies | No QA, some include adult accounts |

### Gaps Consulti can claim
1. **"Built for selling to creators, not sponsoring them."** No incumbent names clippers, editors, growth agencies, or talent managers as its buyer. (Signal of demand: an Apify actor already ships a "YouTube client leads for thumbnail designers" template.)
2. **Bulk export, not rationed unlocks** — loads straight into Bison/Instantly.
3. **Bio keyword include + exclude + adult-content filter** — "clean lists, no junk" vs. Fiverr/low-end lists mixing in OnlyFans accounts.
4. **Pre-built, instant** — no hours-long scrapes, no risk to your IG account.
5. **One credit balance across creators + YouTube/podcast + B2B + local.** Nobody else combines them.
6. **YouTube + podcast DBs = 100% email coverage (~182k).** Only claim 100% for those two, not Instagram.
7. **No contract** (verify Consulti's billing first) — directly answers Upfluence/Aspire/Heepsy complaints.

**Don't:** compete on raw price vs IGLeads/Social Brands; lead with geo targeting; claim "only verified emails" (Influencers.club claims the same).

## 6. Messaging implications

- **Lead with ROI per closed creator.** Examples:
  - Clipping: "Your next $5k/mo retainer is already on this list."
  - Talent: "Sign one unmanaged 500k creator doing ~20 deals/yr and that's ~$24k in commission."
  - Backend: "One 600k creator with a $49/mo community on a 50/50 split = $X/yr."
- **Show the product in the email (Nick Abraham format from the Heinz call):** 3 real creators from the prospect's niche — handle, followers, email — then "I can pull 100 more like this."
- **Frame the big-follower skew as a feature:** "mid-to-mega creators who can actually afford your service."
- **Email vs DMs:** 15–25% email replies vs 3–8% cold DMs; big creators' request folders go unread.
- **Anchor softly:** "tools like Modash charge ~$1.33 per creator email" — run competitor names and $ amounts through the spam checker.
- **Keep the CTA value-first** (free sample of creators in their niche) — it's the top-converting CTA across all past Consulti campaigns (see `consulti-services-and-angles-tried.md`).
