## ANGLES / HOOKS we've tried

**✅ Working (value-first, low-friction):**
- **"Free sample based on your niche"** — the single best-converting CTA (1008, 774, 1009, 1109, 1110)
- **"What are you using for lead data these days?"** — soft question opener (1008, 774)
- **"Owner emails nobody else has"** — local-wedge uniqueness (1009 → 2.57%)
- **"Pull you a list — just tell me who you're after"** — zero-spec, done-for-you feel (1009, 1011)
- **"Who finds your sponsors?"** — creator-monetization pain (1110 → 2.70%)
- **"Gift a month of Pro, nothing owed"** — reciprocity (many)

**⚠️ Weak / flopped:**
- **"Partner / beta cohort — full access for feedback"** — cohort-ask (715/716/647 → 0.15–0.38%)
- **"Founder note — I run the top YouTube channel"** — authority-led open (715 → 0.15%, worst)
- **"Replaces Clay completely"** — tool-replacement as opener (716 → 0.18%)
- **"Deliverability scan"** — problem-hook opener (used mid-sequence in 647/774; not a proven opener)

**Other angles used (mixed / low volume):**
- **Cost/margin nerve** — "who pulls your lists? / your data bill / per-contact bill scales with every client" (1109)
- **Social proof** — "5.4% reply rate (top users, ~3× industry avg)", "2,500+ teams on it" (1109, 366, 647)
- **Cost comparison** — "junior SDR = $60K/yr vs $97/mo" (366)
- **"Run alongside your stack, not rip-and-replace"** — anti-friction reframe (1109 order-7)
- **"Sell TO your audience"** — creator-as-B2B-seller (1110 order-3)
- **Pain opener** — "still finding clients on referrals/word of mouth" (366)
- **Brand authority** — "built by Lead Gen Jay, ~500K subs" (647, 715, 716)

- **HyperCleaner / verification as a standalone opener** (only ever a mid-sequence side-note).

## One-line takeaway for the next build
**Open with a free sample of the data segment that's unique to Consulti (local owners, or creators/podcasts for PR), aimed at agency owners — and keep the cohort/founder/"replace-your-tools" framing out of the first email.**

# Addendum — Creator DB vs B2B DB deep-dive (2026-08-19, live Bison audit)

Verified against Bison workspace 395 and all 737 human replies pulled and read.
Live numbers match the table above (1110 has since ticked to 734 sent / 23 replies / 1 interested).

## Reply rate has been the wrong scoreboard

| Product line | Campaigns | Sent | Replies | Reply% | Interested | Reply→Int | **Emails per interested** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **B2B & Local DB (direct)** | 1009, 1011, 1109 | 9,384 | 178 | 1.90% | 4 | 2.2% | **2,346** |
| **All-in-one platform (B2B embedded)** | 647, 774, 715, 716 | 37,817 | 119 | 0.31% | 10 | **8.4%** | 3,782 |
| **Creator DB** | 1008, 1010, 1110 | 30,710 | 440 | 1.43% | 8 | 1.8% | 3,839 |
| **Account total** | all | 77,911 | 737 | 0.95% | 22 | 3.0% | **3,541** |

The main doc's headline — value-first replies 6–15x better than cohort-ask — is correct and
well-powered (737 reply events). But it measures replies, and replies are not the product.
The "flopped" cohort/founder campaigns converted replies to interest at **8.4%**, roughly
**4–5x better** than the value-first campaigns. On cost-per-interested-lead all three lines
land within 1.6x of each other.

**The angle debate has been arguing over a rounding error.** The account converts at
**1 interested lead per 3,541 emails** against the ~726-per-positive benchmark we sell clients.
That ~5x gap is the actual problem, and no opener fixes it.

## Creator DB — the failure is audience, not copy

30,710 sent, 440 replies, **8 interested**. Campaign 1010 produced 104 replies and **zero**
interested. Reading all 112 human replies, the rejections are about *relevance*, not price,
product, or timing:

> "Influencer outreach isn't a core part of what we're doing for clients right now."
> "This is not applicable to me or my brand."
> "Sorry this does not apply to our business model."
> "I don't 'buy' databases or lists."

We pitched creator data to **general marketing and PR agencies**, who mostly do not run
influencer workflows. That is an ICP error the copy cannot rescue.

The tagged-interested replies confirm it — several are confusion, not intent:

> "Can you tell me why I would reach out to creators, more context?"
> "I am not in need of YT or podcast names, but are you able to create a database of potential
> gets for podcasts if I am already a creator?" *(wants the inverse product)*
> **"Hi its only youtube or other socials as well?"** *(unprompted Instagram demand)*

Our own rep already answered one thread with "Our Instagram database is launching soon" —
so Instagram demand surfaced organically inside this campaign before we ever pitched it.

**Campaign 1110 exposes a second mismatch.** Pitching sponsorship-finding *to creators* hits
people who don't own the decision or don't have the pain:

> "Brands contact me usually, my inbox always has dozens of sellers wanting me to make videos."
> "This one's coming to the wrong inbox. We manage CreditShifu's brand partnerships."

Its 2.70–3.13% reply rate is the highest in the account and is being read as validation. It
isn't — it's 9 human replies, most of them polite deflections.

**1010 should be killed, not paused.** 67% of its human replies were opt-outs or hostile
(three profanity replies, one "I have NOT consented to this CEM… will be reported as spam"),
and it carries the workspace's worst bounce rate at 2.5%.

## B2B & Local DB — smaller reply volume, healthier replies

9,384 sent, 178 replies, 4 interested — the best cost-per-interested in the account at 2,346
emails. The rejections read like ordinary cold-email friction, not product rejection:

> "I have already engaged another company for our needs." *(competitive loss — a real market)*
> "I'm semi-retired and happy to say I'm no longer interested in new leads."
> "I don't own a business."

And the interested replies are concrete, specifying their own ICP unprompted:

> "My two sweet spots are charity organizations and cultural attractions — science museums,
> historical places, zoos, botanical gardens… Directors, Board Members."
> "Sure send me the list." · "Please book a meeting with me."

**Caveat on 1009.** It has the account's best reply rate (2.55%) and its *worst* reply→interest
conversion (0.7%). "Tell me who you're after and I'll pull you a list" is a zero-friction ask
that harvests curiosity, not budget. High reply rate here is a vanity result.

## Three hygiene defects visible in the replies

1. **List decay.** Multiple "I'm retired," "business is closed, sold," "Jay no longer works
   here," "I don't own a business." The B2B/local lists carry dead contacts.
2. **Sender identity mismatch.** 1008 signs "Jay," 1010 signs "Amy Reiner" from
   `consultismart.com`, 1110 signs "Bob" and "Madison." A prospect called it out directly:
   *"why on earth would you think a person would be interested in buying from someone who has
   a DIFFERENT name (very much so) than the 'from'"*.
3. **A rep's own outbound message is sitting in 1011's reply feed** ("Just send me the city and
   type of business…"), so reply counts are marginally inflated.

## Statistical honesty

The reply-rate findings are solid — 737 events. **The interested-rate findings are not.**
Twenty-two interested events across the whole account means any single-campaign comparison
(0 vs 3) is noise. Treat the segment-level direction as a hypothesis to act on cautiously, not
a proven result. Bison's `interested` tag is also applied manually and was unused in this
workspace as recently as 2026-07-21, so the archived campaigns were tagged retroactively and
may be scored on a different bar than the live ones.

## What this changes for the Instagram launch

- **Do not reuse the creator-campaign audience.** The 17,820-contact marketing/PR master list
  is the list that produced 8 interested from 30,710 sends. Instagram data sold to the same
  people will fail the same way.
- **Target influencer/UGC agencies, DTC brands (beauty, apparel, wellness), creator-economy
  SaaS, and talent/music management** — buyers with a creator-contact workflow.
- **The demand signal is already in our inbox** ("is it only youtube or other socials as well?").
  Those repliers across 1008/1010 are the warmest possible seed list for the Instagram launch.
- **Keep the B2B/local wedge running.** It is the better-converting business and needs list
  hygiene, not a new angle.
